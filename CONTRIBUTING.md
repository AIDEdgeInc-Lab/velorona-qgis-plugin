# Contributing data sources to Velorona

Velorona started with Canadian telecom infrastructure data. Global sources, such as satellite data,
are already usable. We welcome suggestions from GIS and telecom professionals for public, licensed
data sources from other countries.

## How to suggest a source

Open a **Suggest a public data source** issue:
https://github.com/AIDEdgeInc-Lab/velorona-qgis-plugin/issues/new?template=data-source.yml

Please include the source URL, the licence or terms-of-use URL, the publisher, and how often the
data is updated.

## What we can accept

- **Public data only.** The dataset must be published openly by its owner, for example a national
  regulator or an open-data portal.
- **Licensed for reuse.** There must be a licence or terms of use that allow the data to be reused.
  Please link to it; we check it before using the data.
- **No private operator data.** Don't submit internal network data, customer data, or anything
  shared with you under a confidentiality agreement. Operators and ISPs who want to use their own
  data with Velorona can contact us separately (see the Velorona Map page). That data is handled
  under agreement and is never published to the public map.

## Review

Every submission is reviewed before anything is merged or added to Velorona. We may ask follow-up
questions, and we may decline a source (for example, if its licence doesn't permit reuse or the data
can't be kept up to date).

## Attribution

We credit contributors publicly **only with their consent**, given by ticking the credit checkbox
on the issue form. If you don't tick it, your suggestion is still welcome and you won't be listed.
You can ask us to remove your credit at any time by commenting on your issue or contacting us.
