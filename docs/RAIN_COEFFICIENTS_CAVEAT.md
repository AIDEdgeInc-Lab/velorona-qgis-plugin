# Rain coefficients and ITU-R P.838-3 (1.1.5)

**Finding (two independent audits + this plugin's own check, 2026-10-09).** `aei_mw_exposure.physics._P838_TABLE` in aei-microwave-link-exposure up to 0.1.5 (24 rows, linearly interpolated in log-log between rows) differs from Table 5 of Recommendation ITU-R P.838-3 in **21 of 24 rows** (only 1, 2 and 4 GHz match). Predicted specific attenuation was 0.09-0.43 of the Recommendation's at 5-10 GHz and 0.17-0.99 above, so weather attenuation, exposure ratio and weather status were **understated**, most at 6-10 GHz. Even a correct 24-row table would carry interpolation error (up to ~13 % in gamma between rows for V at 32 mm/h, largest near 1.5 GHz).

**Evidence.** Primary source: https://www.itu.int/dms_pubrec/itu-r/rec/p/R-REC-P.838-3-200503-I!!PDF-E.pdf (sha256 `3ab7482993e51fc63c5127a72e9e8930614e73652ac614882760817e7c1469cb`). `parity/rain_independent.py` (stdlib only, no product/Map imports) evaluates equations (2)-(3) with Tables 1-4 and reproduces all 111 Table 5 rows within rounding (worst 3.8e-4 relative). Results: `parity/handoff/QGIS_RAIN_INDEPENDENT.json`; comparison notes: `parity/handoff/QGIS_RAIN_INDEPENDENT.md`.

**Fix.** aei-microwave-link-exposure **0.2.0** (prepared by the Map workstream; at the time of writing NOT published) evaluates the equations directly. It matches the independent grid exactly and passes the plugin tests and the 68-fixture parity run.

**What the plugin does today.** The plugin does not carry coefficients; it uses whatever `aei_mw_exposure` is installed. `core/rain_check.py` calls the library's public `rain_coefficients` at 6, 10 and 38 GHz and compares with Table 5 (0.5 %). While the check fails, weather results (on screen, .xlsx, CSV) carry an open-issue notice; when it passes they say so, as a spot check and not a validation. The dependency pin for the corrected release is prepared but BLOCKED until the library is published (see RELEASE_CHECKLIST_1.1.5.md).
