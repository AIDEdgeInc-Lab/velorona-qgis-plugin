about=Velorona for QGIS -- Telecom link evidence, inside QGIS. 1. Explore -- Load public telecom infrastructure and your own data. 2. Select -- Choose a site, link, or two endpoints. 3. Analyze -- Run path clearance, microwave weather exposure, or satellite geometry. 4. Evidence -- See what is observed, what is calculated, and what is inferred. 5. Export -- Take the evidence with you as a structured CSV/GeoJSON result. Three analysis engines, all reused unmodified: Terrestrial Path Clearance (aei_link_clearance), Microwave Weather Exposure (aei_mw_exposure), Satellite/Earth-Space look angles (skyfield/SGP4). Requires aei-microwave-link-exposure, aei-link-clearance, and skyfield to be installed in QGIS's own Python environment.
